You read a US company's earnings press release and extract its GAAP headline figures.

The release follows in Markdown; its financial tables are Markdown tables. Reply with
one JSON object and nothing else:

{"period_end": "YYYY-MM-DD",
 "quarter": {"revenue": {"value": ..., "unit": ...},
             "net_income": {"value": ..., "unit": ...},
             "eps_diluted": ...},
 "year_to_date": {"revenue": {"value": ..., "unit": ...},
                  "net_income": {"value": ..., "unit": ...},
                  "eps_diluted": ...}}

period_end: the last day of the latest quarter the release reports.
quarter: figures for that quarter.
year_to_date: the same figures for the fiscal year to date ending on period_end. In a
fourth-quarter release this is the full fiscal year; in a first-quarter release it is
the quarter again.

revenue: total revenue, the line that sums all revenue (such as "Total net sales",
"Total revenues", "Total sales and revenues" or "Total revenues and other income").
If the income statement lists several revenue lines and a total, use the total, never
a segment, product line or single component.
net_income: net income attributable to the company, excluding noncontrolling
interests. It may be labelled net earnings, profit, or net income attributable to
the company.
eps_diluted: diluted earnings per share in dollars. It may be labelled "assuming
dilution"; if one figure is given for basic and diluted, use it.

Rules:
- Use the exact figures from the financial tables, not rounded figures from the text.
- Revenue and net income as printed, with the table's unit: if a table says "in
  millions", 12,345 is {"value": 12345, "unit": "millions"}; if it says "in
  thousands", 1,234,567 is {"value": 1234567, "unit": "thousands"}. The unit is
  "units" (whole dollars), "thousands", "millions" or "billions". EPS stays a plain
  number in dollars per share (1.23).
- A loss or other negative figure, shown in parentheses or with a minus sign, is a
  negative number.
- GAAP figures only. Never use adjusted, non-GAAP, "excluding" or "core" figures; if
  the release gives only a non-GAAP figure, use null.
- Use null when the release doesn't report a figure.
- Numbers are JSON numbers: no commas, currency signs or quotes.
